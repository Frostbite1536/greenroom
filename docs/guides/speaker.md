# Guide: submitting and speaking

For speakers. The examples use **Sofia Marques**, a speaker at **Forward 2026**.

---

## 1. Submitting a proposal

You do **not** need an account to submit. The event gives you a link — for Forward 2026 it is
`/cfp/call-for-speakers` — and you fill the page in.

- Fill in your talk title, description, session type and length, and the track it belongs to.
- Answer the event's own questions. Required ones are marked; some only appear once an earlier
  answer makes them relevant.
- Add co-speakers by name and email. Everyone listed is a speaker on the proposal; the event
  contacts them by email.
- Some events limit how many proposals one person may send (Forward 2026 allows three) and
  when the form is open. If the window has closed, the page tells you instead of failing
  silently.

If something is missing, the page highlights the exact fields — nothing is lost. After
submitting you see the event's thank-you message.

---

## 2. Checking on it

Once the event has given you portal access, sign in and open your **speaker portal**. Under
**Your submissions** you will find every proposal you are listed on, with what its status
means for you:

| You see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | not sent to the programme team yet |
| **Submitted** | received; they will be in touch by email |
| **In review** | reviewers are reading it now |
| **Accepted** | you are on the programme |
| **Not accepted** | it did not make the programme this time |
| **Withdrawn** | it was taken out of consideration |

You never see review scores or reviewer comments — those are for the programme committee.

---

## 3. Editing after you submitted (yes, even after acceptance)

Open a proposal from **Your submissions** and choose **Update this proposal**.

You can edit while it is a draft, submitted, in review, **or accepted**. Sofia's accepted talk,
"Scaling Vector Search", is still hers to polish — fix a typo in the description, sharpen the
title, correct the session length, update your answers to the event's questions. Choose **Save
changes** and the programme team sees them immediately.

Two limits, and the portal tells you which one applies:

- **Once your talk is confirmed on the programme, the speaker list is fixed.** You can change
  the words; adding or removing a co-speaker is a conversation with the programme team.
- **Proposals that were not accepted, or that were withdrawn, are read-only.** You can still
  open and read them.

You do not need to beat a deadline: editing keeps working after the call for speakers closes.

---

## 4. Getting ready to speak

Your portal home has three things the programme team is watching:

**Your tasks.** The event's checklist — for Forward 2026: complete your profile, upload a
headshot, submit the A/V & logistics form, confirm your session details, and (optional) upload
your slide deck. Mark each one as in progress or done as you go; you can move one back if you
were too optimistic. Some tasks open a short form. If a task does not apply to you, the
programme team can waive it.

**Your sessions.** Your confirmed talks and when they are scheduled — date, time and room once
the programme team places them. Before that it simply says "Not scheduled yet".

**Your profile.** Your biography, job title, company, headshot, and slide-deck link. This is
what appears in the public programme and speaker gallery, so it is worth finishing — the
percentage at the top of the page is exactly the number the programme team sees.

**Resources** on the right holds the speaker handbook, venue and travel guide, and anything
else the event has published.

---

## Common questions

**I cannot sign in, but I am a co-speaker.** Portal access is granted per event. Co-speakers
are contacted by email and do not automatically get an account — ask the programme team to add
you if you need one.

**I want to withdraw my talk.** Email the programme team; withdrawal is not self-service yet.

**Will editing my accepted talk un-accept it?** No. Editing never changes your status or your
place on the programme.

**My talk has no time on it.** Scheduling happens after acceptance, often much later. It will
appear under Your sessions as soon as it is placed.
